# Contact needs ranking

`GET /api/contacts/needs-matches` ranks the authenticated actor's complete contact list against the current `profile.relationshipGoal`. The endpoint is read-only and deterministic. It does not call an AI provider, reuse relationship value scores, write contact state, or accept an actor ID from the client.

`needs-evidence-v2` recognizes Chinese/Japanese/English concepts, including restaurant and ordering synonyms, without scoring conversational fillers. Project context supplies the business scenario, while an explicit counterpart clause supplies specific requested capabilities. A restaurant collaboration goal without a specific role accepts both technical delivery and store-pilot evidence; it does not assume an investor-only or developer-only request.

Ordering includes 点单/點單 and 点餐/點餐 as one concept. Collaboration/implementation evidence must contain a relevant completed action or actual implementation duty in the same source clause. A store identity elsewhere in the profile cannot turn a tax/incorporation landing request into ordering implementation experience. Future plans, requests, negations, and discussion alone do not earn this component; its requested weight remains in the denominator. Mixed factual work and requests retain only the qualifying original source substring. These are conservative Chinese/Japanese/English rules, not a general semantic extractor; the wire family remains `needs-evidence-v2` and fixed product/build SHAs distinguish the correction.

Delivery capability separately requires an actual developer/engineer/store-manager profession, explicit development/delivery duties or performed work, or the contact's stated availability to provide development or store-pilot services. Performed-work and supply clauses also require concrete development/system/implementation/pilot context: “built a marketing campaign” or investor feedback provided to stores is not technical delivery or a store-pilot service. Chinese/Japanese/English requests for developers or pilot customers, future career/learning plans, negations, and a profession mentioned only in a company brand, customer or referral do not establish the contact's ability. The selector uses only original role/profile/relationship/evidence clauses; it never falls back to a keyword elsewhere in the field or in tags. Mixed work and requests retain the qualifying original substring. An explicit own supply claim such as “可提供门店试点场地” is valid capability, but does not establish completed implementation collaboration; these selectors remain independent. A failed capability criterion retains its requested denominator and cannot ground the summary. This is conservative stored-text classification, not independent verification of a claimed profession or service.

The base weights are business scenario 35, specific capability 35, explicit collaboration/implementation 20, and specified location 10. Only dimensions present in the request are normalized to 100. Missing contact evidence never removes a requested weight. Multiple concepts share a dimension's weight; duplicate tags do not multiply points. Original role, profile, relationship and evidence text establish capabilities; names and company brands do not. Structured industry IDs establish their recorded industry, and location requirements require the recorded location. Latin concepts match whole words, not substrings.

General AI references are weak context, with at most 15 points; they cannot dilute or boost specific scenario evidence. Tag-only records and missing requested fields remain `score: null` / `insufficient_data`. Assessable non-matches receive zero. Every match carries actual normalized component weights/points, evidence fields and original excerpts, and a locale-neutral summary code with at most two grounded criterion IDs from distinct dimensions. Components are summed and the final score rounded once. The summary prioritizes specific capability on equal earned weights. This score describes stored-data relevance, not a person's value or a probability of success.

The shared runtime schema validates the v2 component total, expected base weights, original evidence, and summary grounds. App generated contract/schema copies are produced only by `sync:contract`. The UI localizes summary templates and known concept/source labels while keeping original excerpts unchanged. Expanded evidence shows earned/available points for each actual dimension. `needs-lexical-v1` responses remain readable as explicitly legacy keyword scores, with no invented v2 components.

The service reads the profile before and after the contact query. A changed goal/version fails with conflict so a response never combines old scores with a new goal. `goalVersion`, canonical `dataVersion`, and `scoringVersion` bind every response. Contact order, timestamps, and the existing relationship score do not affect `dataVersion`; scoring evidence does.

Known limitation: negated or exclusionary free text returns `needs_clarification` rather than reversing a condition. The client should show the extracted conditions and evidence so the user can inspect the basis.
