# Image Gen 原始提示词

## Editorial

Use case: ui-mockup. Generate ONE realistic, production-quality mobile app UI concept for Orbit, a private relationship/contact companion. One screen only: the contacts home "人脉". User explicitly requests a distinctive NEW visual identity because current generic white/blue rounded-card UI lacks personality. This is authorized broad visual exploration, not a source clone or mere palette swap. The concept must feel ownable through typography, composition, geometry, spacing and person presentation. A usable app, not a poster, landing page, fashion advertisement or feature inventory.
Target dimensions: ONE portrait mobile app content screen 390 x844 logical pixels. Render at high resolution preserving this TALL SLENDER aspect ratio; suitable output 1024 x2216 pixels. No multiple screens, comparison boards or moodboards. App content ONLY: NO device bezel, phone body, notch, Dynamic Island, OS status bar, clock, signal/battery, home indicator, browser chrome, rounded device mask, device shadow, title outside app, option name/number or explanatory annotations.
Attached image1: earlier Orbit relationship analysis UI for PRODUCT CONTEXT ONLY. Attached image2: earlier Orbit navigation drawer for PRODUCT CONTEXT ONLY. Both are prior captured screens, not current screenshots. Do not copy their colors, typography, big padded cards, data, people, analysis panels, AI prose, metrics, drawer structure, platform chrome. We are intentionally exploring a new visual system. Preserve the concept of a PRIVATE personal relationship app, Chinese UI, contacts and card scanning. No backend changes implied.
All three directions use EXACTLY the same core screen content and interactions:
- Product name "Orbit", discreet menu icon providing current app navigation, search icon. Main screen title "人脉".
- Section "最近联系".
- Three FICTIONAL contacts with name, professional identity, short personal context:
"林沐" / "青禾设计 · 产品设计师" / "聊过独立工作室的想法"
"周越" / "远山科技 · 创始人" / "对机器人和新产品感兴趣"
"陈岚" / "栖木空间 · 主理人" / "在设计交流会上认识"
Names and copy exact Simplified Chinese. Any portraits must be generated fictional people, natural candid head-and-shoulders, visually diverse, no famous people. Do not copy photos or names from reference images.
- One clear PRIMARY action "扫名片" with restrained camera icon, in a reachable bottom area. Search and contact rows support the main task. Person entries are tappable with subtle affordance as appropriate to the visual language. NO bottom nav with invented sections; current global navigation accessible via menu.
- No charts, graph, follower counts, arbitrary metrics, social feed, floating AI assistant, tags, pills, notification badges, tutorials, explanation cards, marketing slogans or invented features. No raw email/phone on this home screen.
Date anchor is 2026-09-07. No dates, clocks, years or time-sensitive labels need to be visible. Do not add them.
Layout: focus on people and ONE hero use case. Differentiate by grouping, typography, alignment, purposeful spacing, then thin dividers where needed; borders only when necessary, shadows last. Don't center the whole app as a card on a backdrop. No nested cards, pill-shaped everything or neon-gradient dashboard. Keep all content legible, no clipped text, labels12px minimum, body15-16px, tap areas44px. Use at most TWO typefaces in each concept, permit a meaningful display/body contrast. Each contact's company and context must remain easy to read.
Presentation should be polished enough to judge an enduring PRODUCT design language. This is a real interactive app SCREEN concept, not an editorial cover with controls tacked on. Distinctive, confident, personal, calm, functional. No stock SaaS aesthetic. Do not write the direction name on the image.

DIRECTION NAME: Private Editorial Ledger.
A quiet but unmistakable modern editorial relationship book. Whole surface untextured warm ivory #F0EBDD with near-black #25251F typography and ONE deep vermilion #AF3E2E accent. No blue. Restrained Chinese Song/Ming serif for "人脉" and person names, clean PingFang-like sans for body/meta (two typefaces). Small confidently spaced Orbit masthead paired with thin double-rule detail, compact menu and search icons. Distinctive huge-but-controlled46px serif "人脉" left aligned with a tiny red horizontal locator bar, not a slogan. Section header separated by crisp hairline.
Contact content is a typographic ledger: first contact larger at26px name, elegant large initial "林" in a small off-square ink-outline inset (no portrait required), contextual note set like a short marginal annotation; next two contacts as spacious horizontal ruled entries with names22px, short affiliation and context. Use asymmetric alignment or a consistent narrow marginal rail to feel designed, but preserve clear reading order. Three entries fit without scrolling, 24px page margins, generous whitespace. NO enclosing rounded cards, fake paper texture, torn edges, stamps, doodles or book skeuomorphism. Strong rhythm between fine rules and black serif names. At bottom an ink-black full-width rectangular52px button radius4, white "扫名片" and camera icon, with tiny vermilion edge accent. This screen should feel like a personal collector's index of people, warm, literate, intimate, precise. Final image is ONE tall portrait390x844 screen, not a spread.
